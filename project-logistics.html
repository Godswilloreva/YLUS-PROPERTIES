THE CONCEPT

Movement with
structure.

A logistics hub needs more than warehouse space. Site selection and layout should account for truck access, turning radii, loading areas, storage requirements, security, drainage and links to major transport routes.

Potential components

  • Warehouse and distribution buildings
  • Truck staging and loading bays
  • Vehicle circulation and turning space
  • Staff parking and administrative offices
  • Security, lighting and access control
  • Power, water, drainage and fire safety planning

KEY CONSIDERATIONS

Plan for the whole journey.

Connectivity

Evaluate links to highways, ports, airports, rail or important distribution markets as applicable.

Operational flow

Separate conflicting vehicle movements and plan practical loading and storage operations.

Risk & compliance

Check land use, traffic impacts, environmental conditions, fire requirements and approvals.
