THE CONCEPT

Community by
design.

Residential development should balance plot layout, road access, drainage, utilities, open space and the needs of future residents. Good early planning helps identify constraints before investment in detailed design and construction.

Potential planning components

  • Residential plot layout and access roads
  • Drainage and flood-risk assessment
  • Power, water and waste infrastructure
  • Open spaces and community facilities
  • Security and entrance planning
  • Phased development and maintenance planning

FOUNDATIONS FOR SUCCESS

A community needs more
than buildings.

Liveability

Consider safe access, green space, everyday amenities and the needs of residents.

Infrastructure

Coordinate drainage, roads, utilities and waste systems with the site's conditions.

Documentation

Confirm land title, planning permissions, survey information and development obligations.
