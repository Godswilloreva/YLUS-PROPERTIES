THE CONCEPT

Designed for
operations.

An industrial development concept begins with understanding the intended businesses, logistics needs, site constraints and utility requirements. The layout must respond to real operational needs rather than appearance alone.

Potential planning components

  • Industrial plots and production buildings
  • Internal roads and service access
  • Utility corridors and drainage planning
  • Loading, turning and parking areas
  • Security and controlled entry points
  • Environmental and safety considerations

DEVELOPMENT CONSIDERATIONS

From site to strategy.

Site suitability

Review access, terrain, flood exposure, land use and proximity to transport links.

Infrastructure

Assess power, water, drainage, roads, waste handling and other utility needs.

Feasibility

Examine target occupiers, demand, capital requirements, approvals and delivery risks.
