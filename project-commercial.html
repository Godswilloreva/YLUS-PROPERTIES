THE CONCEPT

Spaces that support
business.

A commercial estate can bring complementary businesses into a coordinated environment. Good planning considers access, visibility, customer movement, parking, utilities and the needs of prospective tenants.

Potential components

  • Retail and showroom units
  • Office buildings and business suites
  • Customer parking and pedestrian routes
  • Service access and loading areas
  • Landscaping and shared public areas
  • Power, water, drainage and security planning

PLANNING PRIORITIES

Commercial value begins
with good planning.

Market fit

Understand the intended users, competitors, local demand and tenant requirements.

Access & visibility

Assess road connections, entry points, parking and movement through the site.

Delivery strategy
